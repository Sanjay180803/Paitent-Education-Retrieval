## Reading these numbers

- **dev** reproduces the README's 91.3%. It is the set the threshold was
  tuned on, so it is not an unbiased estimate.
- **held-out** is perfect, but the corpus is small (37 documents, one page
  per topic), so most answerable questions have a single obvious target.
  This shows the pipeline finds the right document when one exists; it
  does not show it would scale to a larger corpus.
- **adversarial** is the informative set. 9 of 30 near-miss questions were
  answered when they should have been refused:
  - pulmonary hypertension; hypotension (matched high blood pressure)
  - kidney stones (matched chronic kidney disease)
  - chronic fatigue syndrome; sinus infection
  - acute appendicitis guidelines; chronic hepatitis C treatment
    (clinician queries matched unrelated guidelines)
  - ibuprofen with blood pressure medication
  - asthma in horses
  These are the same failure mode as the known insipidus/mellitus case:
  the general-purpose cross-encoder scores topical overlap highly, and
  the 0.01 threshold, chosen to avoid refusing vaguely phrased genuine
  questions, lets them through.
- Jurisdiction and audience violations were 0 across all 113 questions.
